# 10: Answer Replay and staff preview

**What to build:** In review, Answer Replay traces the authored route across the map in step with the audio and highlights the transcript; authored distractor and correction moments are marked. The learner can play, pause, step between route events, jump to a question's part, and choose 0.75x or 1x. With reduced motion the route is drawn at once with numbered steps. Staff preview shows an item exactly as learners will, including Answer Replay.
