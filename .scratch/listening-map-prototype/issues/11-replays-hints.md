# 11: Replays and hints

**What to build:** After the first listen in guided practice, the learner can replay the whole recording or one question's segment and ask for level 1 and then level 2 hints per question. All are recorded as support usage.

**Blocked by:** 08 Guided practice: answer and submit

**Status:** ready-for-agent

Spec: `../spec.md`

- [ ] Replays refused during the first listen; allowed after it, whole or per question
- [ ] Hints come in order: level 1 then level 2; refused in exam mode
- [ ] Support usage (replay count, hint count, hint levels per question) stored on the attempt
- [ ] Learner is told that replays and hints are recorded
