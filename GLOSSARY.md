# Adaptive IELTS Learning

An adaptive practice product for IELTS Academic Reading and Listening, built first for Arabic-speaking learners. Seeded from `ielts_adaptive_learning_product_blueprint.md` v0.1; refine with `/grill-with-docs`.

## Language

### Ways of practising

**Skill challenge**:
One short interactive task aimed at a single micro-skill; feedback appears when it is finished.
_Avoid_: drill, mini-quiz

**Practice set**:
About 4 to 10 related items answered without interruption and reviewed together afterwards.
_Avoid_: quiz, test

**Exam simulation**:
A timed, unseen, unassisted section reviewed only at the end; a full-length version is called a mock.
_Avoid_: mock test (for a section), final test

**Guided practice**:
Practice in which hints and replays are allowed and recorded.
_Avoid_: easy mode, training mode

**Independent check**:
An unseen task done without support under exam-like conditions.
_Avoid_: assessment, placement test

### Progress

**Independent readiness**:
Evidence that a learner succeeds on unseen tasks without support; distinct from accuracy in guided practice.
_Avoid_: score, band estimate

**Support usage**:
The record of hints, replays and other aids used in an attempt.
_Avoid_: assists, cheats

**Mastery path**:
The visible journey of micro-skills and milestones within one question type.
_Avoid_: course, level ladder

**Guided freedom**:
The rule that the product recommends the next challenge but never locks a learner out of any practice.
_Avoid_: gating, unlock

**Provisional recommendation**:
A next-step suggestion built on thin evidence that sharpens with more attempts.
_Avoid_: diagnosis, placement

**Observed error**:
What the learner actually got wrong.
_Avoid_: mistake cause

**Hypothesized cause**:
A possible underlying weakness inferred from several observed errors; never stated as fact from one answer.
_Avoid_: diagnosis, root cause

### Content

**Question type**:
An official IELTS task format, such as map labelling or True/False/Not Given; the main way learners navigate.
_Avoid_: category, exercise type

**Micro-skill**:
A specific underlying ability a challenge trains, such as telling a landmark from a destination.
_Avoid_: topic, lesson

**Item**:
One authored unit of practice content, such as a map with its audio and questions, or a passage with its questions.
_Avoid_: exercise, test (a single prompt inside an item is a **question**)

**Authored route**:
The verified correct path of a Listening map item together with its audio cue times; the only source for Answer Replay.
_Avoid_: generated route, AI route

**Answer Replay**:
Post-attempt review that traces the authored route across the map in step with the audio and transcript.
_Avoid_: playback, solution animation

**Evidence Reveal**:
Post-attempt review that highlights the verified passage span supporting a Reading answer.
_Avoid_: highlighting, explanation

**Published item**:
An item whose answers, evidence and media passed validation and human review; only these reach learners.
_Avoid_: live item, approved item

**Unofficial practice**:
The label every original item carries to show it is not Cambridge or official IELTS material.
_Avoid_: mock exam, official-style test
