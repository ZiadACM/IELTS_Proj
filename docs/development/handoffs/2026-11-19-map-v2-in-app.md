## The rules to build (sources)

- Format: `docs/content/map-format-v2.md` (drawing fields, legs, `word`/`atSec`, correction events with `wrongPath`).
- Owner decisions: D21 to D27 in `docs/product/owner-decisions.md`. D27 is the replay: each point appears at once at its `atSec`; the place just named is highlighted (room area, symbol, or answer letter), one at a time, until the next is named; a new leg starts unlit; a correction's wrong path appears, holds 0.8 s, pulls back over 0.45 s (sine easing), and the walk waits; at the end all answers are framed and numbered, 0.8 s after the last destination. Reduced motion: wrong path dashed and stays; summary not animated.
- Map look: the owner likes it as drawn in the prototype; do not change it.
- Help by mode, not tier (guided practice vs independent check), Arabic explanations, RTL: `CODING_STANDARDS.md`.
